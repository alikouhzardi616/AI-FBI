AI FBI - LICENSE AND PRODUCT INFORMATION

Product: AI FBI
Developer: Ali Kouhzardi

AI FBI is a local-first heuristic security utility. It is provided as-is and
does not replace a maintained antivirus, endpoint detection product, operating
system security controls, backups, or a firewall.

Scanning results are indicators, not proof of compromise. Review findings
before taking action. The software does not execute scanned content.

The optional offline demo and checksum-based activation format are convenience
features only. A checksum is not a cryptographic signature and does not provide
tamper-resistant commercial license enforcement.

See README.md in the installation folder for product scope, build instructions,
and additional limitations.
